Representation Comparison

The tool is scoped to three live branches: STFT, DWT, and Envelope. Mel and Clarke were evaluated and dropped — they appear in the leaderboard for honesty, not as candidates.

STFT

Best solo performer with the modified AlexNet 227 model: 95.39% accuracy and 0.9393 macro-F1. Strong base representation for ensembling.

solo leader

DWT

Plain multi-branch CNN reaches 95.10% accuracy and 0.9358 macro-F1. Native-length sub-band branches; complements STFT well and is part of every top ensemble.

ensemble core

Envelope

Strips the 50 Hz line carrier to expose low-frequency BPFO modulations. Regularized ResNet variant (ENVELOPE_resnet_v3_reg) hits 94.15% accuracy and the highest solo BPFO-3 F1 of any branch (0.823).

bpfo-3 ceiling-breaker
